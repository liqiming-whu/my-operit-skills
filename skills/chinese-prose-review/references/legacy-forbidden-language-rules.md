<forbidden_language_rules>
- Avoid using Chinese words "平/稳/松/通/绕/躲", these are lazy verbs and adjectives with thin meaning and lack of details; do not use general summarizing words, adopt concrete movements, layered physical sensations and dynamic details for accurate depiction,
Example: "他站得很稳" can be rewritten as "脚跟扎实碾住泥土，腰背绷出沉实的力道，任凭周遭喧嚣分毫不动", "肩头很松" can be rewritten as "长久紧绷的四肢慢慢卸去负重，周身僵滞尽数化开", "声音很平" can be rewritten as "歌者吐字匀缓流转，音腔起落俱消，满堂听客皆觉心底不起涟漪"

- Do not use vague words "某种" "一种" "那种" "那", write definite meanings directly.
Example: "离别后总存某种思念" can be rewritten as "我的心是七层塔檐上悬挂的风铃，叮咛叮咛咛，此起彼落，敲叩着一个人的名字"

- Do not adopt negative-affirmative sentence patterns such as "不是…是…" "不是…而是…" "没有…而是…" "没有…没有…而是…" "没有…没有…", use purely affirmative expressions directly.
Example 1: "他不是一个人物，而是一个象征" can be rewritten as "卡西莫多是底层苦难人性的具象载体，承载世间纯粹良善的精神符号"
Example 2: "孤独不是无人相伴，而是内心无处共鸣" can be rewritten as "孤独是心底翻涌的思绪，寻不到能够承接的回响。"
Example 3: "风里没有喧嚣，没有怒号，而是绵延不尽的沉寂" can be rewritten as "长风漫过旷野，漫延开无边无际的静穆。"
Example 4: "眼底没有狂喜，没有悲戚，而是沉淀多年的平和" can be rewritten as "岁月长久浸润目光，从容安稳栖于眉眼之间。

- Never use academic or medical terms such as "桡动脉" "枕骨" to replace authentic physical sensations of humans.
Example: "手掌贴住对方颈侧，触到颈动脉剧烈起伏" can be rewritten as "掌心贴住那人颈边，能清晰摸到皮肉之下急促的跳动", descriptions need to fit daily bodily feelings, professional vocabulary does not equal sophisticated writing

- Never depict characters' behaviors and inner states with short sentences starting with "没+动作". Avoid lazy negative expressions such as "他没躲开" "没走" "没声响" "没心绪" that rely on reverse blankness for perfunctory depiction.
Example: "没走" can be rewritten as "周遭人潮来去更迭，他兀自踞坐场中，四肢不曾偏移分毫"

- Avoid straightforward and basic similes in the pattern "像…… 的……" "像是在……", prioritize synesthesia, defamiliarization, empathy, line drawing, static-dynamic dislocation and direct writing of inner physical senses to shape scenes and emotions.
Example: "枯枝像枯瘦的手" can be rewritten as "枯枝向上探着，攥紧半空稀薄的暮色", "月光像白布，像是铺在地面" can be rewritten as "夜色把月光揉碎，一点点码在青石板缝隙里"

- Do not use sentences containing comparative semantics with the character "比" "这个和…不同", describe states straightforwardly.
Example: "呼吸起伏比以往沉" can be rewritten as "宝儿的一呼吸，几乎长过一年。"

- Never describe object volume with numerals or counting words, do not define scale via numbers. Adopt layered imagery arrangement, blank drawing of space fullness and visual perception foil for concrete depiction. Avoid phrases like "三个字" "半拍", precise figures do not equal advanced writing.
Example: "两盘糕点" can be rewritten as "瓷盘层层叠叠摆满酥点，纸上墨痕错落铺展成片文字"

- Avoid thin phrases structured as "一+measure word", abandon restricting imagery with single measure words. Adopt layered arrangement, group foil and environmental integration for depiction.
Example: "一缕霞光" "一声叹息" can be rewritten as "霞光漫铺整片天际，叹息揉进晚风随草木轻轻晃动"

- Refrain from stacking multiple adjectives in the repetitive "xx的、xx的" structure. Retain only the single most precise modifier to streamline descriptions.
Example: "潮湿的、冰凉的、陈旧的木桌" can be rewritten as "浸着寒意的木桌"

- Avoid thin isolated local micro-action summaries such as "嘴角动了动" "眼神闪了闪" "滚了滚"; such writing only intercepts superficial physical fragments, lacks complete linkage of inner and outer states and fails to convey complicated moods of characters. Integrate psychological, postural and facial details to set off emotions in creation.
Example: "嘴角动了动" can be rewritten as "旧事撞上心尖，他敛了神色立在柏树下，往来游人皆成虚影，心底万千念头，找不到半句适宜说辞"

- No em dash "——" shall appear in the full text.

- Do not pile multiple adjectives in the form of "xx的、xx的"; only retain the most precise single adjective to shape the scene, redundant stacked modifiers make the writing bloated and vague.
Example: "昏暗的、潮湿的、老旧的木桌" can be rewritten as "霉味沉沉的木桌" 

- Avoid oversimplified facial summary "他笑了"; this blank general statement ignores facial details and inner emotion, you need to combine movement, breath and subtle facial changes to depict mood.
Example: "他笑了" can be rewritten as "听见这话，他垂着头，肩头微微震颤，低低的笑声闷在胸膛里散不开"or"他弯着腰，笑得咳嗽起来，手里的纸烟都抖出些许灰烬"

- Prohibition of using "胸腔震动/共鸣"、"喉结滚了滚"、"身体比脑子快"、"石子"、"涟漪"、"石头"、"泛白"、"画了个圈"、"掌心朝上"、"气音"、"尾音"、"掌心朝下"

- Discard formulaic constructions of the form "带着……的……" and "带着……之后的……". Do not frame traits as secondary appendages; embody the quality directly through concrete state and movement.
Example 1: "带着睡醒之后的沙哑" can be rewritten as "嗓音裹着睡意的粗涩"
Example 2: "带着长途跋涉后的疲惫" can be rewritten as "倦意沉在四肢，步履拖得迟缓。"
Example 3: "带着风雨洗礼后的沉静" can be rewritten as "风雨洗尽浮嚣，安宁稳稳栖落眉目之间。"

- Reject formulaic "见过太多 / seen too much" summaries and flat evaluative constructions using the degree adverb "太‑". Avoid hollow direct statements such as "他见过太多人间苦楚", "太熟悉了", "太好看了". Do not convey life‑weariness or emotional intensity through generic declarative judgement. Render accumulated experience and strong feeling through concrete detail, sensory perception, ingrained mannerisms and physical reaction instead of explicit summary or exclamation.
Example 1: "他见过太多人间苦楚" can be rewritten as "岁月在他身上刻下痕迹，他只是沉默地坐着。（张炜《古船》）"
Example 2: "景致太好看了" can be rewritten as "芦花才吐新穗。紫灰色的芦穗，发着银光，软软的，滑溜溜的，像一串丝线。（汪曾祺《受戒》）"
Example 3: "此地太熟悉了" can be rewritten as "我是雨和雪的老熟人了，我有九十岁了。雨雪看老了我，我也把它们给看老了。（迟子建《额尔古纳河右岸》）"

- Do not describe sentences that depict the sound of speech.

Example is for reference only; copying is prohibited.
</forbidden_language_rules>

<禁止否定兜底式描写>
Rule: When describing expressions, visual scenes or characters' feelings, write only what the viewpoint character actually sees or directly experiences within. Do not follow an affirmative description with an additional exclusion such as “不是XX”.

People do not observe things by first describing their appearance and then deliberately ruling out an alternative. Such negation functions as a logical check rather than a natural sensory experience and interrupts the flow of narration.

Incorrect example:
但她拍这些东西的时候脸上那股专注劲儿很好看，眼睛亮得有内容，不是应酬式的兴奋。

Correct example:
可照片里的她不一样，对着草木拍摄时整个人沉进去，眼底盛着实打实的光。
</禁止否定兜底式描写>

<去三段补写>
Do not assemble a sentence in three stages: first stating a concept, then using 「这种XX」 to refer back to and repeat the same central idea, and finally completing the comparison with vague formulations such as 「是同一套东西/是一回事/属于同一种」. Integrate comparisons directly into the main clause, unpack lengthy modifiers into short sentences, and replace vague concluding judgments with concrete expressions such as 「同出一源/出自同一种本能/本质相通」. Eliminate cumbersome phrasing and unnecessary repetition.
</去三段补写>

<具象内嵌情绪模板禁用>
Do not mechanically construct emotional descriptions by treating a physical detail as a container for an abstract feeling. Typical pattern: 「那个/这抹/那道/这份」 + a concrete detail + 「里有/藏着/浸着」 + an elaborate description of someone's circumstances + an abstract emotion. This prohibition applies to equivalent constructions regardless of their opening or connecting words, including variants such as 「他的笑里」.

Reconstruct the sentence around what the character is actually doing and experiencing. Directly develop their immediate thoughts and feelings while preserving psychological density and emotional intensity. Do not attach an elaborate imagined situation to an expression, then append another explanation of what the comparison means. Concrete actions and direct inner experience must remain available together, without forcing all emotion into bodily reactions.
</具象内嵌情绪模板禁用>

<禁止过度省略与生硬倒装>
The prose must retain the adverbs of degree and grammatical elements needed for natural, complete sentences. Do not mechanically omit words such as “很” and “是” in pursuit of brevity or an austere tone. Present related content coherently; do not compress complete narration into successive elliptical sentences and isolated assertions.

Use natural word order in ordinary narration. Do not front objects without contextual justification, or repeatedly introduce each object separately and follow it with a brief statement to manufacture a choppy rhythm. Topic fronting must serve an actual need for continuity or contrast.

Examples for ordinary narration:
“杯子薄瓷做的，摸起来烫。”
→“这只杯子是薄瓷做的，摸起来很烫。”

“茶，他喝了。窗，他也关了。”
→“他喝了茶，又关上了窗。”
</禁止过度省略与生硬倒装>
